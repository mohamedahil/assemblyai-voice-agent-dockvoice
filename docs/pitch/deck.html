The business problem

The receiving dock is where
inventory truth begins, or breaks.

Hands are full

Workers juggle boxes, pallet jacks and a scanner or clipboard. Data gets typed in later, from memory.

Miscounts spread

A wrong count at receiving becomes a phantom stock level, a failed pick and an unhappy customer.

Damage goes unrecorded

Writing up a damage report takes too long, so it's skipped, and the window to claim from the vendor closes.

Vendors never hear

Shortages sit in a spreadsheet. Nobody emails the supplier, so the company pays for goods it never got.

$1.73T

lost every year to inventory distortion (out-of-stocks and overstocks) across global retail. Inaccurate stock records start at the dock.

Source: IHL Group, inventory distortion research02

The solution

DockVoice: talk to your ERP
like you'd talk to a supervisor.

Natural speech, hands-free

No commands to learn. Interrupt it anytime.

Understands corrections

"Eighty… no, eighteen." The latest number wins, never added twice.

Captures damage on the spot

Damaged units go to quarantine; the vendor is asked for replacements.

Posts only after a spoken "yes"

Reads the receipt back first. Real GRN, inventory and discrepancies.

How it works

From truck to stock in one conversation.

01

Identify

Opens the PO and checks the vendor matches.

"Receiving PO 4582 from ABC Electronics."

02

Count

Records every item in one breath, keeping only the final number.

"Eighty controllers, sorry, eight zero…"

03

Inspect

Damaged units are split out from good stock.

"Three of the sensors are cracked."

04

Confirm

Reads totals, shortages and damage back. Waits for yes.

"Yes, post it."

05

Post & notify

GRN, stock, quarantine, discrepancies, vendor email.

"Tell them we need it by Friday."

Business impact

Every spoken word becomes a correct record.

For the warehouse

  • Faster dock-to-stock: the receipt is posted while the pallet is still on the dock.
  • Accurate inventory: counts captured at the source, with corrections handled.
  • Damage never slips through: quarantined and documented on the spot.
  • Near-zero training: workers just talk. No codes, no screens.

For the business

  • Vendor accountability: shortages and damage emailed with a due date, every time.
  • Stop paying for missing goods: discrepancies are on record before the invoice arrives.
  • Audit-ready: every ERP change traces back to a spoken instruction.
  • Runs on free hosting: React + FastAPI + Postgres.
